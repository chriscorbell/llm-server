### XMX data types

Xe2 XMX supports FP16, BF16, INT8, INT4 and INT2 (per HWCooling's Xe2 analysis; 2048 FP16 ops/clock and 4096 INT8 ops/clock per Xe core). BF16 runs at the FP16 rate (367 INT8 TOPS implies about 183 TFLOPS BF16 dense on B70). **Native FP8 is not present on Xe2 XMX.** HWCooling's Xe3 analysis says even Xe3 only adds FP8 dequantization, and Intel's Xe3P (Crescent Island) is the first with FP8/FP4 matrix support. Intel's datasheet only quotes INT8 TOPS. Practical consequence: FP8 checkpoints on B70 are dequantized or handled via software paths; vLLM XPU's `--quantization fp8` works (dynamic), but community testing on B70 reports "FP8 block currently lacks an optimized XPU scaling kernel in vLLM" and that INT4 (GPTQ, `XPUwNa16LinearKernel`) is the fast path.

Sources: [HWCooling Xe2](https://www.hwcooling.net/en/batttlemage-details-of-intel-xe2-gpu-architecture-analysis/), [HWCooling Xe3](https://www.hwcooling.net/en/xe3-whats-new-in-panther-lakes-gpu-architecture-analysis/), [VideoCardz Xe3P](https://videocardz.com/newz/intel-details-xe3p-gpu-architecture-crescent-island-gets-up-to-480gb-memory-and-350w-pcie-variant), [SergiioB B70 cookbook](https://github.com/SergiioB/intel-arc-pro-b70-inference-cookbook/blob/master/docs/qwen38-27/QWEN38-VLLM-XPU.md).

### Linux driver status

- **Kernel:** Intel's Xe KMD table lists Arc Pro B70 (PCI ID 8086:e223) and B65 (e222) as fully supported in-box from kernel 6.17 (Ubuntu 25.10+); B60 (e211) from 6.15, B50 (e212) from 6.14. No `force_probe` is needed on 6.17+, so kernel 7.0 works out of the box. Phoronix tested four B70s on stock Ubuntu 26.04 with Linux 7.0 and Mesa 26.0.3, and found further Xe driver performance gains on 7.1 (OIDN, OSPRay SYCL). Kernels older than 6.17 (e.g. 6.12) lack the PCI ID entirely and `force_probe=e223` does not help.
- **Intel's Ubuntu recommendation:** Intel says use in-box Ubuntu drivers ("You don't have to install anything from any of our custom repositories") and pick Ubuntu 26.04+ for Battlemage. dgpu-docs offers two extra paths for Arc Pro B-series: the kobuk-team `intel-graphics` PPA (newer packages, not to be combined with OMIX) or **Intel Open Middleware Xe (OMIX)**, a pinned validated stack. OMIX 0.2.0 was validated on B70 with Ubuntu 24.04 + 6.17 HWE and ships oneAPI DPC++ 2026.0.0, oneDNN 2026.0.0, oneMKL 2026.0.0, oneCCL 2022.0.0.
- **Compute runtime:** intel/compute-runtime lists Battlemage as "Production" (OpenCL 3.0, Level Zero 1.17). Latest release 26.31.39395.13 targets Ubuntu 26.04 with kernel 7.0.0-14-generic; 26.14.37833.4 is the last one Phoronix used (with IGC 2.32.7).

Sources: [dgpu-docs Xe KMD GPU table](https://dgpu-docs.intel.com/overview/supported-hardware/xe-driver-gpus.html), [dgpu-docs installation paths](https://dgpu-docs.intel.com/installation-guides/index.html), [Intel article 000091844](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/support/articles/000091844/graphics.html), [OMIX 0.2.0 notes](https://dgpu-docs.intel.com/overview/release-notes/OMIX/0.2.0.html), [compute-runtime releases](https://github.com/intel/compute-runtime/releases), [Phoronix 4x B70 on Ubuntu 26.04](https://www.phoronix.com/review/intel-arc-pro-b70-four), [Phoronix Linux 7.1 B70](https://www.phoronix.com/review/linux-71-arc-pro-b70), [ZimaOS issue #491](https://github.com/IceWhaleTech/ZimaOS/issues/491).

### Known compute issues on Battlemage Linux

- **Resizable BAR:** Intel says ReBAR is not required but is needed for best performance on Arc. Community testing reports compute throughput cut roughly in half without it (256 MB aperture). Proxmox passthrough users report a B70 BAR only exposing 16 GB and xe probe hangs with ReBAR on some platforms; disabling ReBAR was their workaround. ([Intel 000092416](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/support/articles/000092416/graphics.html), [Intel Community thread](https://community.intel.com/t5/Graphics/ARC-PRO-B70-Passthrough-Hell-on-PVE-Resizable-BAR-Problems/m-p/1748878), [HackingPC guide](https://hackingpc.com/hardware-builds/intel-arc-gpu-guide-2026/))
- **4 GB single-allocation cap:** Level Zero refuses single allocations over ~4 GB by default on Arc; set `UR_L0_ENABLE_RELAXED_ALLOCATION_LIMITS=1`. This bites llama.cpp KV caches at 32K+ context on 30B models. ([Unified Runtime L0 docs](https://oneapi-src.github.io/unified-runtime/core/LEVEL_ZERO.html), [Hal9000AIML B70 fixes](https://github.com/Hal9000AIML/arc-pro-b70-ubuntu-gpu-speedup-bugfixes))
- **Multi-GPU:** compute-runtime #922 (multi-rank Level Zero crash after upgrading 26.05 to 26.14 on Ubuntu 26.04/xe, unresolved, stale); vLLM #41663 (TP=2 GP fault + xe BCS engine reset on dual B70, Ubuntu 24.04 HWE 6.17, workaround `CCL_ENABLE_SYCL_KERNELS=0`). A field guide says TP needs compute-runtime 26.14+ paired with kernel 7.1-rc6+ for a multi-root USM fix, plus triton-xpu 3.7+, and oneAPI 2025.3 (2026.0 failed at profile_run). ([#922](https://github.com/intel/compute-runtime/issues/922), [vLLM #41663](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/41663), [chriswagner-ai guide](https://github.com/chriswagner-ai/intel-arc-b70-vllm-multi-gpu))
- **llama.cpp SYCL:** hybrid GDN/Mamba models had a recurrent state-leak bug on SYCL (second response answers first question), MoE slot-init hangs, wrong Xe2 warptiles in Vulkan; `SYCL_CACHE_PERSISTENT=1` causes segfaults. Vulkan is the safer backend for hybrid models. ([bentech benchmarks](https://bentech.substack.com/p/intel-arc-pro-b60-b70-llm-benchmarks), [llama.cpp #22885](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/issues/22885))

## B) Qwen3.8 family and Qwen3.8-27B

### What exists

"Qwen3.8" is real. Official Qwen HF repos: `Qwen/Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B` (+FP8, MoE flagship, Aug 12), `Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B` (+FP8, Aug 14, 2026), `Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next` (+FP8, Aug 24; 125B total, 6B active, plus 51B n-gram embeddings and a 4B MTP, previewing the Qwen4 architecture with Qwen Sparse Attention). There is **no Qwen3.8-35B-A3B or 122B-A10B**; those sizes belong to Qwen3.5. Apache 2.0.

Sources: [HF API listing](https://huggingface.co/api/models?author=Qwen&search=Qwen3.8), [Qwen3.8-27B card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B), [Flash-Next card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next), [2.4T-A95B card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B), [Qwen on X](https://x.com/Alibaba_Qwen/status/2092591393424515114).

### Qwen3.8-27B architecture (from config.json)

- Dense, 27.78B params, `Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration` / `model_type: qwen3_5` (same architecture as Qwen3.5/3.6-27B, so any engine supporting qwen3_5 works).
- **Vision is native** in the same checkpoint (27-layer ViT, hidden 1152, patch 16, image and video tokens); `--language-model-only` drops it.
- 64 layers, hidden 5120, FFN 17408, vocab 248,320. **Hybrid attention:** `layer_types` = 48 `linear_attention` (Gated DeltaNet: 16 K heads, 48 V heads, dim 128) + 16 `full_attention` (Gated Attention: 24 Q heads, 4 KV heads, head_dim 256), interval 4.
- **KV cache:** only the 16 full-attention layers have a growing cache: 2 x 4 heads x 256 dim x 16 layers = 32,768 values/token, i.e. 64 KB/token at BF16, 32 KB at FP8. The 48 GDN layers hold a fixed recurrent state. (Quesma measured about 2.3 GB per 32K tokens at F16 in llama.cpp, consistent with this.)
- **MTP head:** yes, `mtp_num_hidden_layers: 1`, not dedicated embeddings. vLLM: `--speculative-config '{"method":"mtp","num_speculative_tokens":3}'` (also `qwen3_5_mtp`), 0.75+ acceptance; on B70 vLLM XPU, 93 to 96% acceptance at 4 tokens single-stream, dropping to 43 to 56% under 5-way concurrency.
- Context: 262,144 native; YaRN to ~1,010,000 (static factor, may hurt short prompts).
- Thinking mode default on with `<think>`, `enable_thinking: false` per request, `reasoning_effort` low/medium/xhigh (default xhigh), `preserve_thinking` on by default. Sampling: thinking T=1.0/top_p 0.95/top_k 20; non-thinking T=0.7/top_p 0.8.

Sources: [config.json](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B/resolve/main/config.json), [vLLM recipe](https://recipes.vllm.ai/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B), [SergiioB cookbook](https://github.com/SergiioB/intel-arc-pro-b70-inference-cookbook/blob/master/docs/qwen38-27/QWEN38-VLLM-XPU.md).

### Official quantized releases and serving

- Official from Qwen: **FP8 only** (`Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B-FP8`, block size 128, "nearly identical" metrics). No official AWQ or GGUF; GGUFs are community (unsloth, bartowski, etc.). Qwen's card lists SGLang (cookbook), vLLM (recipe, needs 0.17.0+, transformers 5.8+; `--reasoning-parser qwen3`, `--tool-call-parser qwen3_coder`, `--kv-cache-dtype fp8`), TokenSpeed, Transformers, plus llama.cpp/Ollama/LM Studio.
- Sources: [FP8 card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B-FP8), [vLLM recipe](https://recipes.vllm.ai/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B).

### Quantization and engine quality issues

- Quesma (llama.cpp, Aug 16 build): Q4_K_M matches BF16 on GPQA Diamond, IFBench and Terminal-Bench 2.1; Q2 drops noticeably; IQ1 collapses to chance. ([Quesma](https://quesma.com/blog/qwen38-27b-quantizations-benchmarked/))
- GGUF conversion strips the MTP head, so llama.cpp GGUFs cannot use MTP speculative decoding; "MTP-ONLY" community GGUFs exist as a workaround. ([SergiioB](https://github.com/SergiioB/intel-arc-pro-b70-inference-cookbook/blob/master/docs/qwen38-27/QWEN38-VLLM-XPU.md), [llama.cpp discussion #27164](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/discussions/27164))
- llama.cpp issue #27756 (open, Aug 26): Qwen3.8-27B silently emits EOS past ~130K prompt tokens on CUDA and CPU, suspected GDN recurrent-state accumulation error scaling with 48 GDN layers. ([#27756](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/issues/27756))
- vLLM: MXFP4 does not load on NVIDIA; on B70 XPU, GPTQ-Int4 works (52 to 84 tok/s decode single-stream with MTP, 1.7K tok/s prefill, ~18 GB weights), AWQ and NVFP4 do not, FP8 block is unoptimized, and long-context MTP needs two community patches. ([vLLM recipe](https://recipes.vllm.ai/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B), [SergiioB](https://github.com/SergiioB/intel-arc-pro-b70-inference-cookbook))
